# A05 · 千问 · 思考过程

Let me calculate the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) for this company.

Given information:
- Market value of equity (E) = 600亿元
- Market value of debt (D) = 400亿元
- Cost of equity (Re) = 10%
- Pre-tax cost of debt (Rd) = 5%
- Corporate tax rate (T) = 25%

The WACC formula is:
WACC = (E / (E + D)) × Re + (D / (E + D)) × Rd × (1 - T)

Step 1: Calculate total value (V)
V = E + D = 600 + 400 = 1000亿元

Step 2: Calculate weights
Weight of equity = E / V = 600 / 1000 = 60%
Weight of debt = D / V = 400 / 1000 = 40%

Step 3: Calculate after-tax cost of debt
After-tax cost of debt = Rd × (1 - T) = 5% × (1 - 25%) = 5% × 0.75 = 3.75%

Step 4: Calculate WACC
WACC = 60% × 10% + 40% × 3.75%
WACC = 0.6 × 0.10 + 0.4 × 0.0375
WACC = 0.06 + 0.015
WACC = 0.075 = 7.5%
