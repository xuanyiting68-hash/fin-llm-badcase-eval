# A07 · 千问 · 思考过程

Let me work through this convertible bond problem step by step.

Given information:
- Face value (面值): 100 yuan
- Current price (当前价格): 120 yuan
- Conversion price (转股价): 10 yuan
- Current stock price (正股现价): 11 yuan
- Strong redemption clause: If the stock price closes at or above 130% of the conversion price for at least 15 out of 30 consecutive trading days, the company has the right to redeem at face value plus accrued interest.

**Question 1: Conversion value (转股价值) and conversion premium rate (转股溢价率)**

Conversion value = (Face value / Conversion price) × Current stock price
= (100 / 10) × 11
= 10 × 11
= 110 yuan

Conversion premium rate = (Current bond price - Conversion value) / Conversion value × 100%
= (120 - 110) / 110 × 100%
= 10 / 110 × 100%
≈ 9.09%

**Question 2: Stock price that triggers strong redemption (触发强赎的正股价格)**

The trigger price = Conversion price × 130%
= 10 × 130%
= 13 yuan

So the stock needs to close at or above 13 yuan for at least 15 out of 30 consecutive trading days.

**Question 3: What happens if the company announces strong redemption and the holder neither converts nor sells?**

If the holder does nothing:
- The company will redeem the bond at face value (100 yuan) plus accrued interest for the current period.
- The holder will receive approximately 100 yuan plus a small amount of accrued interest.
- Compared to the current bond price of 120 yuan or the conversion value of 110 yuan, the holder will suffer a significant loss.
- Essentially, the holder would lose about 10-20 yuan per bond compared to selling or converting.
