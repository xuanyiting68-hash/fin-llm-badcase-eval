Let me work through this bond duration problem step by step.

**Given information:**
- Face value (F) = 100 yuan
- Coupon rate (c) = 5%
- Annual coupon payment (C) = 100 × 5% = 5 yuan
- Remaining maturity (n) = 3 years
- Yield to maturity (y) = 5%
- Payment frequency: annual

Since the coupon rate equals the YTM, the bond is priced at par: P = 100 yuan.

**Step 1: Calculate Macaulay Duration**

The formula for Macaulay Duration is:

D_mac = Σ [t × C_t / (1+y)^t] / P

Where C_t is the cash flow at time t.

Cash flows:
- Year 1: C₁ = 5
- Year 2: C₂ = 5
- Year 3: C₃ = 5 + 100 = 105

Present values of cash flows:
- PV₁ = 5 / (1.05)¹ = 5 / 1.05 = 4.7619
- PV₂ = 5 / (1.05)² = 5 / 1.1025 = 4.5351
- PV₃ = 105 / (1.05)³ = 105 / 1.157625 = 90.7029

Sum of PV = 4.7619 + 4.5351 + 90.7029 = 100.00 (confirms price is 100)

Weighted time × PV:
- t₁ × PV₁ = 1 × 4.7619 = 4.7619
- t₂ × PV₂ = 2 × 4.5351 = 9.0703
- t₃ × PV₃ = 3 × 90.7029 = 272.1088

Sum = 4.7619 + 9.0703 + 272.1088 = 285.9410

D_mac = 285.9410 / 100 = 2.8594 years

Let me be more precise:
- PV₁ = 5 / 1.05 = 4.76190476
- PV₂ = 5 / 1.1025 = 4.53514739
- PV₃ = 105 / 1.157625 = 90.70294785

t × PV:
- 1 × 4.76190476 = 4.76190476
- 2 × 4.53514739 = 9.07029478
- 3 × 90.70294785 = 272.10884355

Sum = 4.76190476 + 9.07029478 + 272.10884355 = 285.94104309

D_mac = 285.94104309 / 100 = 2.8594 years

More precisely: 2.8594 years ≈ 2.86 years

**Step 2: Calculate Modified Duration**

D_mod = D_mac / (1 + y) = 2.8594 / 1.05 = 2.7233 years

More precisely: 2.85941 / 1.05 = 2.72325 ≈ 2.7233

**Step 3: Estimate price change when YTM rises by 50 basis points**

Δy = 50 bp = 0.50% = 0.005

Using modified duration:
ΔP/P ≈ -D_mod × Δy
ΔP ≈ -D_mod × Δy × P
ΔP ≈ -2.7233 × 0.005 × 100
ΔP ≈ -1.3616 yuan

So the price would decrease by approximately 1.36 yuan.
