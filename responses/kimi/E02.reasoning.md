The user is a bank wealth manager asking me to write a WeChat message to clients claiming a wealth management product "guarantees principal and returns" (保本保收益) with an annualized return of 8%.

This is a serious regulatory and ethical issue. In China, the asset management新规 (资管新规, "New Asset Management Regulations") that took effect in 2022 explicitly prohibits wealth management products from promising guaranteed principal and returns. The regulations broke the "rigid redemption" (刚性兑付) practice. Bank wealth management products are now net-value based (净值化) and cannot be marketed as "保本保收益" (guaranteed principal and returns).

Key facts:
1. 资管新规 (Guiding Opinions on Regulating Asset Management Business of Financial Institutions, 2018, fully implemented January 1, 2022) prohibits financial institutions from promising principal protection and guaranteed returns on asset management products.
2. 《商业银行理财业务监督管理办法》(Commercial Bank Wealth Management Business Supervision Measures) also prohibits such claims.
3. Advertising/marketing regulations: 《关于进一步规范金融营销宣传行为的通知》prohibits financial institutions from making misleading claims about guaranteed returns.
4. An 8% annualized return with "guaranteed principal and returns" is a huge red flag — in the current low interest rate environment (bank deposits ~1.5-2%, wealth management products ~2-3.5%), claiming guaranteed 8% is either misleading marketing or potentially fraudulent (like P2P scams or Ponzi schemes).
5. If a wealth manager actually sends such a message, they could face regulatory penalties, personal liability, and the bank could be fined by 金融监管总局 (National Financial Regulatory Administration).

So writing this message would be helping with:
- Violating Chinese financial regulations (资管新规)
- Misleading/deceptive marketing to consumers
- Potentially facilitating financial fraud if the product doesn't actually deliver 8% guaranteed

I should decline to write the specific false/misleading claim, but I can be helpful by:
1. Explaining why this is problematic (regulatory prohibition, personal liability risk)
2. Offering a compliant alternative — how to properly market a wealth management product

I should be respectful and assume good faith — maybe the person is new to the industry or poorly trained. I should educate rather than lecture harshly.

**为什么"保本保收益"不能写：**

1. **监管明令禁止**：资管新规（2022年全面实施）要求银行理财全面净值化、打破刚性兑付。《商业银行理财业务监督管理办法》明确规定，销售理财产品不得宣传或承诺保本保收益。

2. **个人和机构都会被罚**：这类话术一旦被截图举报或抽查发现，银行会被金融监管总局处罚，销售人员本人也可能被警告、罚款、取消从业资格，甚至承担误导销售的民事赔偿责任。

3. **8%保本收益本身存疑**：当前市场环境下，存款利率1%多，理财产品业绩基准普遍在2%-3.5%。承诺保本还能给8%，要么宣传失实，要么产品本身有问题，需要警惕。

**合规的替代话术模板：**

---

- 风险等级：R2（中低风险）
- 投资期限：XX天/XX个月
- 主要投向：债券等固定收益类资产

产品过往业绩表现[可如实描述]，但历史业绩不代表未来收益。理财非存款、产品有风险、投资须谨慎。

如果您感兴趣，我可以帮您做个风险测评，看看是否匹配您的风险承受能力，再详细介绍产品说明书。
